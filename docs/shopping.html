Budget

Small fridge? The first option is all you need. RS485 is only worth it when sensors are many metres from the ESP.

You already have the ESP32, 5 V 5 A supply, relays and atomizer board, so those cost nothing. Prices in USD. dac.ge items converted at about 2.7 GEL per dollar. Each AliExpress seller charges its own shipping (about $5-9), so order from as few sellers as you can.

Notes

Why not the DHT11? It is rated 0-50 °C and 20-90 %RH (±5 %), so it can't measure a cold room near saturation. Keep it for desk testing.

Why XY-MD03 and not XY-MD02? The classic XY-MD02 uses an SHT20, and its manual rates it to 0-80 %RH. The XY-MD03 uses an SHT40 (0-100 %RH) on a waterproof probe and runs on 5 V.

Before wiring them together: every sensor ships as address 1. Connect them one at a time and set addresses 1, 2, 3... in the web UI (Sensors → RS485 / Modbus tools).

Small parts with no link: a 120 Ω resistor (for the far end of the RS485 cable), a PC817 optocoupler and 330 Ω resistor (only if the atomizer board needs a button press), wire, and heat shrink.

Water: use demineralised or RO water in the ultrasonic misters, otherwise white mineral dust settles on the apples. Buy it locally.
